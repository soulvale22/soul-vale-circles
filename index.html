Intimate Relating

Come home to yourself
to meet one another.

Sunday, November 22 · 5–7pm EST

Every relationship we have begins with the relationship we have with ourselves.

The way we meet ourselves shapes the way we meet others. When we learn to listen to our bodies, recognize what's true for us, honor our needs and desires, and remain present with what we're feeling, we begin to relate from a different place.

Rather than looking outside ourselves to feel whole, we discover the fullness that already exists within us. From here, connection becomes less about seeking something from another and more about sharing, exploring, and experiencing life together.

In this circle, we'll gently turn inward before turning toward one another. Through somatic exploration, embodied presence, and authentic sharing, we'll explore the many dimensions of intimate relating: seeing and being seen, listening and being heard, expressing and receiving, opening while honoring our boundaries, and allowing ourselves to be met without abandoning ourselves.

What becomes possible when we can stay connected to ourselves while allowing another person to come closer?

This is an invitation to slow down, soften, become curious, and explore intimacy as a living experience rather than something we have to achieve.

Because the deeper we come into relationship with ourselves, the more fully we can meet one another.
